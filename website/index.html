Inside XpandMax / v0.9.2

A little more context.
A lot less clicking.

A compact line below each author adds the context you need without taking over the post. Follower counts, a short bio, and a follow button stay together.

Make yourself at home on X ↗
01

Stay in the conversation.

See blue follower counts and a short bio below post authors. Expand only when you want the full bio and profile location. Supported Chrome versions can translate foreign-language bios into Chinese on your device.

02

Follow without the wait.

A follow button sits beside the follower count for authors you do not follow. One click uses X’s own follow action, with a clear result.

03

A quieter notification icon.

Hide the unread number on X’s notification bell. It is on by default; restore the number anytime in the extension popup.

04

Small footprint. Private by design.

Uses data X has already loaded. No tracking, no extra account lookups, and no data sent to us.
